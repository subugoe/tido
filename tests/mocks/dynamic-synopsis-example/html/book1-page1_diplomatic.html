Call me Ishmaël. Some yeares ago—neuer mind how long preciſely—hauing little or no money in my purſe, and nothing particular to intereſt me on ſhore, I thought I would ſail about a little.

Whenſoeuer I find myſelf growing grim about the mouth; whenſoeuer it is a damp, drizzly Nouember in my ſoul, I account it high time to get to ſea as ſoon as I can.

There is nothing ſurpriſing in this. If they but knew it, almoſt all men in their degree cheriſh very nearly the ſame feelings towards the great water with me.
