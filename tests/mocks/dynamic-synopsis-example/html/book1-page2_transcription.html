Loomings — Page 2 (Transcription)

It is a way I have of driving off the spleen and regulating the circulation. This is my substitute for pistol and ball, and the loomings of the voyage return to me whenever the land grows narrow.

Now, when I say that I am in the habit of going to sea whenever I begin to grow hazy about the eyes, I do not mean to have it inferred that I ever go to sea as a passenger.

For to go as a passenger you must needs have a purse, and a purse is but a rag unless you have something in it. No, when I go to sea, I go as a simple sailor, right before the mast.
